Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by MyTravelExpert HQ
Customer Trust Guarantee: We treat cancellations fairly and transparently. We do not impose hidden penalty fees beyond non-refundable vendor/hotel commitments.

1. Standard Cancellation Slabs

In the event that you must cancel your booking, the following refund schedule applies based on the date written notice is received by our desk via email (infomytravelexpert4306@gmail.com) or verified WhatsApp (+91 8001247778):

Notice Period Prior to Tour Start Refund Percentage of Total Tour Cost Retention / Operational Charge
30 days or more 90% Refund 10% administrative processing fee
15 to 29 days 75% Refund 25% non-refundable hotel advance
7 to 14 days 50% Refund 50% hotel and vehicle lock-in
Less than 7 days / No-Show No Cash Refund 100% retention (Credit Voucher options may apply)

2. Bhutan SDF & Non-Refundable Govt Fees

For Royal Bhutan packages, the Royal Government of Bhutan's Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) and official visa processing charges are governed by the Department of Immigration of Bhutan and may be non-refundable once approved.

3. Weather, Landslides & Force Majeure

If your tour is interrupted or truncated midway due to unforeseen natural occurrences (landslides, heavy snowfall, bridge closures, or political bandhs):

  • Unused nights at partner hotels will be refunded subject to the individual property's waiver confirmation.
  • Vehicle retention fees remain applicable as mountain drivers remain deployed with your party for safety.
  • We offer a Travel Credit Voucher valid for 12 months for any unutilized package portions.

4. Refund Processing Time

All approved refunds are credited back to the original source bank account / UPI / credit card within 5 to 7 business days. A formal refund credit statement and bank reference number (UTR) are emailed immediately upon dispatch.
