Booking Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by MyTravelExpert HQ
Transparent Reservations: Every reservation with MyTravelExpert is confirmed in writing with an official Booking Voucher bearing your trip reference code (e.g. #MTE-XXXXX) and detailed hotel/cab vouchers.

1. How Booking Works

We believe in straightforward, reliable expedition planning:

  • Step 1 — Consultation & Customization: Select from our 16 curated Himalayan packages or configure your dates, group size, and vehicle category via our online quote builder or WhatsApp desk (+91 8001247778).
  • Step 2 — Proforma Invoice & 10% VIP Discount: You will receive a detailed proforma invoice reflecting your guaranteed 10% VIP discount and day-wise route breakdown.
  • Step 3 — Advance Deposit: Secure your hotels, dedicated vehicle, and permit slots with a 20% to 30% advance deposit.
  • Step 4 — Final Voucher Issue: Upon receipt of the deposit, we issue your confirmed Hotel Booking Vouchers, Driver Contact Details, and Permit Check-In Guidelines.

2. Payment Schedule

  • Advance to Confirm: 20% of total package cost at the time of reservation.
  • Pre-Arrival Balance: 30% payable 15 days prior to arrival date (applicable for peak season: April–June & October–December).
  • Arrival Balance: Remaining 50% payable upon pickup at Bagdogra Airport (IXB) or NJP Railway Station before departure into the hills.

3. Accepted Payment Methods

For safety and accounting integrity, we accept:

  • Official Bank NEFT / RTGS / IMPS transfer to our verified current accounts.
  • UPI Payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM) to our registered business VPA.
  • Major Indian and International Credit / Debit Cards (via secure payment gateway link).

4. Flexible Date Rescheduling

We understand that unforeseen family or health events can occur. MyTravelExpert offers Free Date Rescheduling up to 14 days prior to your original travel date, subject to seasonal hotel rate parity.
