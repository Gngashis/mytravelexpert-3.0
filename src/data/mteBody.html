What documents are required for North Sikkim, Nathula Pass, and Bhutan permits?

For Tsomgo Lake, Nathula Pass, and North Sikkim (Lachung/Yumthang), Indian nationals need a valid Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License plus 2 passport photos (Aadhaar is not accepted for restricted permits). For Bhutan, Voter ID or Passport works—our team processes all permits for you.

00

Do you provide pickup and drop-off from Bagdogra Airport (IXB) and NJP Railway Station?

Yes! Because our headquarters is located right in Bagdogra (Bhujiapani, M.E.S. Colony), our driver and local representative greet you directly at Bagdogra Airport (IXB) or NJP Station with zero wait time.

00

What types of vehicles do you provide for mountain roads?

We operate a dedicated fleet of 13+ mountain-ready vehicles including Toyota Innova Crysta, Mahindra Scorpio, Xylo, Bolero, Maruti Ertiga, WagonR, Dzire, Force Tempo Traveller (13–26 seater), and Luxury Coasters driven by experienced hill drivers.

00

What is the best time of year to visit Darjeeling, Sikkim, and Bhutan?

March to June offers pleasant weather and blooming rhododendrons in Yumthang Valley, while October to February brings crystal-clear Kanchenjunga views, festive Bhutan Tshechus, and high-altitude snow at Zero Point and Nathula.

00

Which luxury hotels and resorts do you partner with?

00

Is it safe for senior citizens and young children to visit North Sikkim and Silk Route?

Yes, with proper acclimatization! We pace our itineraries carefully, provide high-clearance luxury SUVs, carry portable oxygen cylinders for high-altitude passes, and have 24/7 local support on standby.
